About Me

I was born in Amasya in 2000. I completed my primary and middle school education in Gelibolu, then went to high school in Amasya. I study Geomatics Engineering at İzmir Katip Çelebi University and expect to graduate in 2027. I now live in Amasya, where I work as an intern geomatics engineer at Gökçe Harita Mühendislik. My main academic interest is GNSS, because accurate positioning is the foundation of every map and survey.

Outside of engineering, I am a musician. I have played the electric guitar for more than ten years, performed in various bands, and stepped on stage more than 500 times during my university years.
